What Do I Say?

A practice tool, not a crisis service. Nothing here replaces a counsellor, a doctor or a trusted adult. If someone is in immediate danger, call 112. Built as an IB MYP Personal Project.

Getting real help

This tool is for practising. If you or someone you know needs support right now, these lines are free, confidential and staffed by trained people.

Tele-MANAS
14416
Government of India national mental health helpline. Free, 24 hours, multiple languages. Also on 1800-891-4416.
KIRAN
1800-599-0019
Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. Free, 24 hours, 13 languages.
Emergency
112
If someone is in immediate physical danger, call emergency services first.

Tell a trusted adult. A helpline does not replace someone in your actual life. Your school counsellor, a teacher you trust, or a parent can do things a friend cannot. Telling them is not betraying anyone.
